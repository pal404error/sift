Why Sift?

Building generative search shouldn't mean vendor lock-in. Sift provides a powerful, pluggable foundation with a clean API, built-in crawl orchestration, and enterprise SSO—all while staying completely open for your custom embedding and LLM providers.

🔌 Pluggable Architecture

Swap out vector stores, LLMs, and embedding models with simple config changes. Out-of-the-box support for the heavy hitters, completely open for custom providers.

🛠️ Developer Experience

Clean FastAPI endpoints, a powerful `sift` CLI, and an instant static web UI. We sweat the details so you can ship faster.

🕷️ Crawl & Evaluate

Built-in crawl orchestration to keep your data fresh, and offline evaluation pipelines to ensure your retrieval and generation stay sharp over time.

🔐 Enterprise Ready

Deploy securely on your own infrastructure. First-class OIDC/SSO integration, robust audit logging, and deep health metrics.
